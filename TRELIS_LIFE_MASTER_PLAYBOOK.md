2. **The "Thin-Fat Indian" Phenotype (Sarcopenic Obesity):** In the South Asian and broader urban demographic, standard BMI metrics are dangerously deceptive. Sarcopenic obesity—marked by low skeletal muscle mass, high visceral fat, and severe insulin resistance despite a "normal" body weight—is a silent epidemic. Genetics and environment conspire to drive elevated inflammatory profiles, high cardiovascular risk, and severe clinical anomalies (such as the founder's initial homocysteine panel reading a hazardous **32.61 µmol/L** compared to the optimal clinical baseline of `<8 µmol/L`).

The market's response has been to dump more data on the user. Between continuous glucose monitors (CGMs), smart rings (like Ultrahuman), and metabolic wristbands, users are drowning in diagnostic metrics while starving for daily behavioral structure. **You cannot out-exercise a bad diet, and you cannot out-medicate loneliness.** What people need is not a dashboard of their failures; they need a quiet, resilient trellis to support their daily growth.

### The Moat (Behavioral Science): Differentiating from the Noise
While competitors like **Ultrahuman** pivot to clinical diagnostics panels and **HealthifyMe** introduces generative AI nutritionists, Trelis Life occupies a vacant, high-value position: **a silent, local-first daily structure tool**. 

Our behavioral moat is anchored in three specific scientific principles:

#### 1. Implementation Intentions (Type C Stacks)
Based on Peter Gollwitzer's cognitive research, simple goals ("I will eat healthier") fail because they demand constant executive function. Trelis Life bypasses the prefrontal cortex by utilizing **Type C Stacks** (Behavioral Chaining). By linking a highly specific supplement protocol directly to an existing physical cue (e.g., *"Upon entering the kitchen at 06:00, take the Probiotic + NAC stack"*), the friction of decision-making is reduced to zero. 

#### 2. The Streak Paradox (Forgiveness Beats Punishment)
Duolingo-style streaks drive short-term compliance but long-term resentment. When a user breaks a streak due to illness, travel, or an emergency, they experience a "streak-shame event," leading to permanent app abandonment. Trelis Life resolves this with a tri-layer forgiveness engine:
- **Pause Tokens:** Users are granted two non-purchasable Pause Tokens per calendar month. Burning a token converts a missed day into a neutral **Rest Day**, preserving their streak.
- **Sprout-to-Flame Visuals:** Early streaks are labeled as a fragile, growing sprout (`🌱`). Once a 7-day threshold is crossed, it transforms into an active flame (`🔥`), acknowledging deep habit crystallization.
- **The Lifetime Done Ledger:** While streaks can reset, the **Lifetime Completed Anchors** count is a permanent database trigger that never resets. The user's historical identity is protected forever.

#### 3. Zero Confetti (The World-Class Calm Aesthetic)
There are no animations purely for decoration, no slots-style sounds, and no behavioral notifications. Trelis Life is designed with a premium, low-arousal color palette (warm paper `#F7F6F2` canvas, pure white elevated `#FFFFFF` surfaces, and a single deep teal `#2A7F7F` accent). It is built to feel like a high-end piece of medical hardware—an intentional ledger that you open, log in under 3 minutes, and close to go live your life.

### The Morning Hook (06:00 - 10:00 local time)
*Time required: 30 seconds.*
1. **The Intention:** Upon waking, open the app. The screen will display your **Vision Priming Card** over a soft solar gradient background. Read your core daily focus aloud to anchor your subconscious.
2. **The Fasted Stack:** Head to the kitchen. Take your morning fasted stack (e.g., your probiotic and cellular support supplements). Tap the stack in the app.
3. **The Morning Gratitude:** Log one simple, 1-sentence gratitude entry in the morning reflection card.
4. **Result:** You have locked in **10% of your Pulse Score** before your workday even begins.

### The Engine (Midday / Afternoon)
*Time required: 60 seconds.*
This represents the **70% core** of your daily metabolic score:
1. **Circadian Sunlight Exposure:** Step outside between 10:30 and 14:30 for 10–15 minutes. The direct lux photons hitting your retinas suppress melatonin production, locking in your night-time sleep drive.
2. **The Protein Threshold:** Ensure you hit your metabolic amino acid target (e.g., 1.5 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight, divided across high-quality meals containing sufficient leucine to trigger protein synthesis).
3. **The 20-8-2 Desk Rule:** For every 20 minutes of seated work, stand for 8 minutes, and move for 2 minutes to combat postural muscle stagnation and maintain endothelial health.

### The Evening Wind-Down (Post 21:00)
*Time required: 60 seconds.*
1. **The Caffeine Cutoff (14:00 strict):** Ensure all caffeine intake stops by 2:00 PM. While standard clinical studies suggest a 6-hour window, Trelis enforces a conservative 14:00 cutoff to protect deep sleep phases in sensitive nervous systems.
2. **The Supplement Closer:** Take your **Deep Sleep Stack** (e.g., 400mg Magnesium Glycinate + 1000mg Taurine) to down-regulate the sympathetic nervous system. 
3. **The Core Non-Negotiable:** Perform your daily fiber/intestinal clearance protocol (such as the daily **Psyllium Husk** anchor). This is marked with a persistent red-dot UI tag and has no streak forgiveness.
4. **The Three-Line Reflection:** Spend 20 seconds completing the Evening Reflection:
   - **The Win:** What went right?
   - **The Drain:** What pulled energy?
   - **Tomorrow:** What is the single highest-leverage priority?

Trelis Life is built as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). You do not need to wait for App Store approvals to experience full native performance:
1. Open the application on your iPhone using the Safari browser.
2. Tap the **Share Icon** in the bottom navigation bar of Safari.
3. Scroll down and select **Add to Home Screen**.

Every complex active anchor (Type A) in Trelis Life is configured with a **Baseline Threshold**. 
- *The Full Anchor:* "Cardio Session (45 Minutes)"

If you are exhausted, traveling, or injured, **do not skip the habit**. Drop down to the baseline trigger. The system grants you half-credit, keeps the cognitive neural pathway active, and protects your streak while respecting your body’s need to down-regulate.

### Total Airplane Mode Resiliency
Unlike modern cloud-dependent apps that fail on a spotty connection, Trelis Life features a dual-layer client-first sync engine. 
- The app caches all anchors, reflections, streaks, and scores in a local **Zustand cache** backed by native device preferences.
- You can board a 14-hour flight, log your supplements, write your journal entries, and review your metrics in full offline mode.
- The moment your phone re-establishes a network connection, the background sync engine silently negotiates with our Supabase Postgres cloud, merging database records with microsecond accuracy. Zero loading screens. Zero friction.
