# Welcome to Trelis Life — User Manual

This guide will show you how to use **Trelis Life**. 

Trelis Life is not a typical health app. It does not track complicated numbers or try to stress you out with notifications. Instead, it is a **Life Operating System** built to help you bring calm, quiet structure to your day.

## 1. The Trelis Philosophy
Before using the app, it helps to understand how Trelis works:
*   **Less is More:** You can only have up to 4 personal habits (called "Anchors") active at one time. This keeps you focused.
*   **No Stress Metrics:** We do not display raw counts like "10,000 steps" on the main screen. Instead, we show simple checkboxes and progress rings.
*   **Calm Colors:** The app uses clean, soft colors. Inactive options turn gray so they do not distract you.
*   **Haptic Vibrations:** The app uses light vibrations (on mobile devices) to make interactions feel premium and satisfying.

## 2. First-Time Account Creation & Log In
When you open Trelis Life at `/login`, you will see a clean sign-in screen.

### Fields and Options:
*   **Email Input Box:** Type your email address here.
*   **Password Input Box:** Type your password here.
*   **Create Account / Log In Buttons:**
    *   If you already have an account, enter your email and password, then tap **Log In**.
    *   If you are new, tap **Create Account**. You will be asked to type your password a second time to confirm it.
*   **Confirm Password Box (Sign Up mode only):** Re-type your password to make sure you did not make a mistake.
*   **Forgot your password?** Click this if you cannot remember your password. It will send a recovery email.
*   **Grayscale Compliance Message:** A small note at the bottom explains that Trelis is a wellness app, not a medical doctor.

## 3. The Onboarding Setup Screen
Once you create an account, you will enter a 6-step setup screen (`/onboarding`).

*   **Step 1: Set Windows:** Set the times you usually wake up and go to sleep. Use the time selectors (e.g., `07:00` and `23:00`).
*   **Step 2: Meet The Arc:** Read about "The Arc"—your permanent daily cycle.
*   **Step 3: Breaking Loops (Habit 1):** Set your first habit. You can give it a title (like "Do hard task first"), choose a secret "Mission Codename" (like "Operation Daybreak"), and write a "Keep Definition" (what counts as successfully done).
*   **Step 4: Body-Mind (Habit 2):** Set your physical/mental habit (like "Daily Walk" or "Stretching").
*   **Step 5: Stillness or Reset (Habit 3):** Set your recovery habit. You can choose "Stillness" (resting, breathing) or "Reset" (cooking, writing, exercising).
*   **Step 6: Preview & Seed:** Review your habits. Tap **Enter Trelis Life** to save them and open your dashboard.
*   **Skip Option:** If you do not want to set these up now, tap **Skip** in the top right corner. You can set them up later.

## 4. The First-Time Guided Tour
The first time you land on the Dashboard, an interactive guided tour automatically starts. It highlights elements on the screen one by one with a spotlight.

*   **Step 1: Morning & Evening Windows:** Highlights the wake and sleep target timers.
*   **Step 2: The Arc Keystone:** Highlights your central daily card.
*   **Step 3: Seam & Arc Progress Ring:** Explains how the circle fills as you complete habits.
*   **Step 4: Personal Anchors:** Shows the list of your custom habits.
*   **Step 5: Daily Progress Dots:** Shows how your week is tracking.
*   **Step 6: Quiet Partner:** Highlights the silent companion window.
*   **Step 7: Finish:** Tapping **Hide it** completes the tour so it won't show again. Tapping **Skip** closes it but lets it show next time.

## 5. The Dashboard (Your Main Screen)
This is where you spend your time. It is divided into clear sections.

### The Header Bar:
*   **Streak Score ( 🌱 or 🔥 ):** 
    *   Displays your current daily streak.
    *   If your streak is under 7 days, it shows a **Sprout 🌱** to keep it low-pressure.
    *   If you reach 7 days or more, it transforms into a **Flame 🔥**.
    *   If you are starting over, it says "Fresh Arc".
*   **Pause Tokens:** Displays how many streak-saving tokens you have left (you start with 2 per month).
*   **Resting Indicator:** Shows if you have activated a rest token today.

## 7. The Arc Card (Morning & Evening Rituals)
This is the main card at the top of your dashboard. It cannot be deleted.

### Part A: Morning Check-In (Tap when you wake up)
*   **Wake Time Button:** Shows what time you woke up.
*   **Feet-on-Floor Checkbox:** Check this as soon as you get out of bed.
*   **Sleep Quality (1 to 5):** Tap stars/circles to rate your sleep.
*   **Morning Mood (1 to 5):** Tap stars/circles to rate how you feel.
*   **Morning Tap Button:** The button at the bottom. Click it to submit your morning data.

### Part B: Evening Wind-Down (Tap before sleep)
*   **Win of the Day Text Box:** Write one good thing that happened today.
*   **Parking Lot Text Box:** Write down any worries or to-do items to clear your mind.
*   **Bedtime Log:** Click to log when you go to bed.

### Info Button ( ⓘ ):
*   Tap the small **Info icon** next to "The Arc" title to read a quick explanation of how it serves as your daily anchor.

## 8. Personal Anchors (Your Habits)
Below The Arc Card, you will find your custom habits. 

### How to Check Off:
*   Tap the checkbox next to any habit to mark it **Done**. The box turns teal, and the title gets crossed out.

### Gathering Context (Sprout Button):
Next to the checkbox is a small leaf/sprout icon. Tapping it opens the **Gather** pop-up:
*   **Label Field:** Type a short word or detail about how you did this habit (e.g., "Park run" instead of just "Run").
*   **Tags:** Select preset labels to tag your entry.
*   **Submit:** Saves this context. It does not allow long-form writing to prevent stress.

---

## 9. Quiet Partners (Silent Support System)
The **Quiet Partner** card lets you stay connected with a friend without social pressure.

### How it Works:
*   It displays your partner's email address.
*   **Progress Dots:** Shows exactly 6 dots representing their active habits. Green/teal dots mean they did them; gray dots mean they did not.
*   **No Numbers:** You cannot see their names, their streak numbers, or their exact notes.
*   **Nudges:** Click one of the pre-set buttons to send a silent note:
    *   `Thinking of you`
    *   `Proud of you`
    *   `Want to walk?`

## 10. Trellis Garden (Visual Growth Indicator)
At the bottom of your screen is the visual **Trellis Garden**.

*   It displays a climbing vine for each of your active habits.
*   **Green Nodes:** Every time you complete a habit, a green leaf grows on the vine.
*   **Missed Days:** Missed days appear as a thin gray thread.
*   **Flowers & Blooms:** Logged gathers (context logs) cause beautiful flowers and leaves to blossom on that habit's vine.

## 11. The Sunday Weekly Debrief (Weekly Review)
The `/debrief` page is gated. It is locked from Monday to Saturday.

### Gated Lock Screen:
*   If you visit before Sunday, you will see a lock icon: `"This portal opens on Sunday."`

### Sunday Workbook:
On Sunday, the workbook unlocks:
1.  **Drain Box:** Type what drained your energy or focus this week.
2.  **Win Box:** Type what went well.
3.  **Change Box:** Type the one change you will make for next week.
*   **Submit Button:** Tap **Commit Weekly Reflection** to log it. This creates a special Sunday reflection record.

## 12. Settings Portal (`/settings`)
Access settings via the navigation link to customize your account.

### Sections:
*   **Guided Tour Section:** Tap **Replay Onboarding Tour** to start the 7-step spotlight tour again on the dashboard.
*   **Morning Tap Line Config:** Choose or type what text appears on your main check-in button (e.g. `"I'm here. Let's begin."`).
*   **Vision Priming:**
    *   **Toggle Switch:** Turn Vision Priming on or off.
    *   **Morning Intention Text Area:** Write a sentence to remind yourself why you do this (e.g. `"Focus on what brings clarity."`).
*   **Manage Anchors Panel:**
    *   Create a new anchor: Select a template or start from scratch. Type a title, optional codename, frequency, and "Keep" definition.
    *   **Edit:** Modify existing anchor parameters.
    *   **Retire:** Turn off an anchor. It will move to the retired section.
    *   **Activate:** Re-enable a retired anchor.
*   **Partner Management:**
    *   **Invite Email Field:** Type a friend's email to send them a Quiet Partner invitation.
    *   **Remove Partner Button:** Disconnect a partner.
*   **Nuclear Reset (Sign Out):** Purges local storage cache and signs you out.

## 13. Helpful Tips & Concepts

### A. How Pause Tokens Work
*   If you fail to check off your anchors for a day, the system automatically uses **1 Pause Token**.
*   This marks the day as a **Rest Day 🌙** instead of resetting your streak.
*   You get 2 Pause Tokens per calendar month. They reset on the 1st of the month.
*   If you have 0 tokens and miss a day, your active streak resets to 0.

### B. What is a "Keep"?
*   A "Keep" is the bare minimum action needed to count a habit as done. For example, if your habit is "Eat Healthy", your Keep might be: `"I ate 1 serving of vegetables."` Even on your worst day, keeping just this promise counts.

### C. What is a "Gather"?
*   A "Gather" is the leaf or flower you add to your habit. It tracks simple details (like where you walked or who you called) to add positive memory markers without keeping a heavy, stressful diary.
